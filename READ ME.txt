Stills & Reels 2.2
==================
(formerly Picture Preview)

1. Right-click StillsAndReels.zip > Extract All...
2. Double-click StillsAndReels.exe. There's nothing to install.
3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" > "Run anyway".
   (This appears because the app isn't code-signed.)

Following clicks anywhere (new)
- Pictures mode now also picks up a picture you click on a web page or in another app.
  It is copied from the screen exactly as shown there (same size as on screen), because
  the app never downloads anything. For the full-size original, right-click the picture
  in your browser and choose "Copy image": copied pictures are picked up at full quality.
- Files you click on the desktop or in an Open/Save dialog of any app are followed like
  File Explorer clicks: pictures in Pictures mode, videos in Videos mode and the video editor.
- Files you copy anywhere (Ctrl+C) are followed too.
- Turn each of these on or off in Settings > Following. "Follow clicks and File Explorer
  selection" in the View menu still switches all following off and on.
- Pictures from web pages and copied pictures are never taken while you are in Edit picture,
  and nothing is picked up while the video Snip window is open.
- Clicks are only watched, never blocked or changed. The app doesn't record which
  keys you press or anything about the pages you visit.

Sharper video (new)
- Video snip records the PC's own sound (videos, music, games, calls) by default; the PC sound
  button in the Video snip bar turns it off. It works on its own or mixed with a microphone, and
  records whatever plays on your default speakers or headphones. Your Video snip choices (PC
  sound, microphone, frame rate, pointer) are remembered.
- Sound and picture in a recording are lined up more tightly. The screen capture starts before
  the sound; the app now times how long after the first frame the sound actually began and
  shifts the sound by exactly that, so a slow start-up (for example a microphone that takes a
  moment to open) no longer leaves the sound ahead of the picture or ending before it.
- Each recording writes a short technical log to %LOCALAPPDATA%\StillsAndReels\recording-log.txt
  (what FFmpeg reported and the measured picture lead). It contains no picture or sound, and you can
  delete it at any time.
- Screen recordings are captured losslessly while you record, then encoded once in high
  quality when you press Stop ("Finishing the recording… 42%" shows while that runs).
  Small text and thin lines stay crisp, and pausing no longer costs quality.
- The screen is captured with Windows Desktop Duplication: whole frames straight from the
  graphics card, so playing video doesn't tear and far fewer frames are dropped. Where that
  isn't available (a remote desktop session, an area that spans two screens) the older
  capture method is used instead.
  While recording, the lossless parts are kept in the cache and can be large when the screen
  shows a lot of movement (a full-screen video can take about 1 GB a minute). They are
  deleted once the recording is finished, and recording stops by itself, keeping what it
  has, if the disk gets nearly full.
- Colours stay true: exports and recordings are written as standard HD colour (BT.709) and
  labelled so, so players no longer shift reds, oranges and greens.
- Videos in the editor are decoded with sharper scaling, and a clip that fills the canvas is
  copied pixel for pixel instead of being resampled.
- The first video you put on a new project sets the canvas size and frame rate to match it,
  so a 4K or 60 fps clip (or a 1366 × 768 screen recording) is no longer squeezed to 1080p 30.
- Export offers the project's exact size and frame rate (chosen by default), and the quality
  choices now run Smaller file, Standard, High (default) and Best.

What's new in 2.2
- The app is now called Stills & Reels.
- Your settings carry over from Picture Preview automatically on first run.
- The old Picture Preview video engine and cache folders are cleaned up on first run
  (as long as the old app isn't open), so you don't keep two copies of the engine.
- Video projects now save as .srproj. Your existing .ppvproj projects still open,
  and saving one keeps its original name and extension.
- You can delete the old StillsAndReels.exe once you're happy with the new one.

What's new in 2.1
- Pasting into Edit picture (like paint.net): Ctrl+V or the Paste button in the sidebar.
  The picture floats as a layer: drag it, drag its handles to resize, press Enter or click
  outside to place it, or press Esc to cancel. With no picture open, Ctrl+V starts a new picture.
  Ctrl+Alt+V always pastes as a new picture.
- Snip copies every capture to the clipboard straight away, and has a
  "Paste into editor" button (Ctrl+E) that drops the snip into Edit picture.
- Edit > Edit video (Ctrl+Shift+E), or the Edit button next to Videos: the video editor.
  A video playing in the viewer goes straight onto the timeline.
- Edit > Video snip (Ctrl+Alt+R), or Snip next to the video Edit button: screen recording.

Video editor at a glance
- Left: Media (import, or click videos in File Explorer), Text, Transitions, Filters, Effects.
- Centre: preview. Drag the selected clip to move it, drag a corner to resize,
  drag the round handle to rotate.
- Right: properties of the selected clip (Basic, Adjust, Effects, Audio, Speed or Text).
- Bottom: timeline. Drag clips to move them (also onto a new track), drag their ends to trim.
  Space plays, Ctrl+B splits, Del deletes, Shift+Del deletes and closes the gap,
  Ctrl+D duplicates, Ctrl+K adds a keyframe, Ctrl+wheel zooms, Ctrl+Z undoes.
- File > Save video project (Ctrl+S) saves a .srproj file. It remembers where your files are.
  Older .ppvproj projects also open.
- Export (top right, or Ctrl+M): MP4 (H.264 + AAC) at the project's size or 480p-4K, at the
  project's frame rate or 24-60 fps, or MP3 sound only.

The media cache
- Every video you view (and every file you import) is copied into a private cache folder
  for the editor. Copies stay until you remove them from Media or close the app, then
  they are deleted. You can turn off keeping viewed videos in Settings.
- Recordings, freeze frames and pasted pictures also live in the cache. When you save a
  project, they are copied into a "<project name> media" folder next to it.

Privacy
- Pictures are read from a temporary copy that's deleted when you move on.
- Edits, snips, recordings and exports are only ever saved as new files.
  Originals are never changed or overwritten.
- Nothing is sent anywhere. The app has no network code.

Video engine
The first time you use a video feature, the app unpacks its built-in video engine (FFmpeg 7.1)
to %LOCALAPPDATA%\StillsAndReels\Engine (about 85 MB, takes a few seconds, once only).
The app starts it in the background; you never run it yourself. It is only given local
file paths (and, when you record, your screen, the microphone you choose and the PC's sound,
which the app passes to it over a private local pipe), so it never
uses the network. To remove it, delete that folder.

Stills & Reels' own code
------------------------
The application code (C#, about 17,000 lines) was written for you by Claude (Anthropic's
AI assistant) during this project. No source code, icons or other assets were copied from
any other project, repository or product:
- All icons are simple line drawings made in code.
- The look follows the reference image you supplied.
- The image effects use standard, published formulas rather than anyone's code:
  luma weights from ITU-R BT.709, the YCbCr conversion from ITU-R BT.601 (chroma key),
  saturation and hue-rotation matrices in the style of the W3C Filter Effects specification
  (feColorMatrix), the widely used sepia colour matrix, and textbook box blur, unsharp
  mask and vignette techniques.
